Common questions

How do I contact a seller? Tap the green Call button on a listing. Some listings only have contact details in their description; tap "more" to read it.

Do I need an account? No. Browsing, calling and sharing work without one. Sign in with Apple or Google only to like and comment.

How do I report a comment or block someone? Open the comments, tap ⋯ next to the comment, then choose Report or Block this person.

How do I delete my account? Tap your photo at the top right, then Delete account. See the privacy policy for other ways.
