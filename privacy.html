Privacy Policy – पसल किनबेच

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Pasal Kinbech Pvt. Ltd. ("we") publishes the पसल किनबेच (Pasal Kinbech) Android app, which shows businesses for sale and lease from our Facebook page and from public business-listing websites.

Browsing without an account

You can browse listings, call sellers and share listings without signing in. The app has no ads and no analytics.

If you sign in

Signing in with Google lets you like listings and post comments. We then store, in Google Firebase (servers in Mumbai, India):

DataWhyVisible to others?
Name and profile photo from your Google accountShown next to your commentsYes, with your comments
Email address and an account IDTo sign you inNo
Your commentsSo others can read themYes
Your likesTo show like counts and your ♥Only as a total count
People you blocked; comments you reportedModerationNo

We don't sell this data or share it with anyone except Google, which hosts it for us.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: tap your photo (top right) → Delete account. This immediately removes your account, your likes, your comments and your block list.

Without the app: email pasalkinbech206@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my account". We delete the account and all its data within 30 days. Comment reports you filed are kept for up to 90 days for moderation, without your name.

Network requests

To show listings the app downloads pages and photos from the public listing website it reads listings from, and from our feed hosted on GitHub. These servers see your IP address and device type as part of any normal web request.

Calls and sharing

"Call" opens your phone's dialer with the seller's number; nothing is dialled until you press call. "Share" opens Android's share sheet.

Children

The app is meant for adults buying or selling businesses and is not directed at children.
